Map view: rough mockups

The maps are drawn by hand and the data is made up. The real app would use real, zoomable vector maps (MapLibre + OpenStreetMap) in light and dark. These are about layout and what you see at each time scale, not final polish.

Route colours: walk · cycle · drive · train · flight. Pink dots are places you stopped.

1A day

Map fills the screen. Day / Week / Month / Year switch at the top, arrows (or swipe) to step through time. The sheet at the bottom is the day's timeline; drag it up for the full list, down to see more map. Tapping a row flies the map to that stop or route.

3A trip

A stretch of days away from home, shown as one map: flights as arcs, ground travel as routes, cities sized by nights spent. The sheet gives the trip's numbers and a day-by-day summary; tap a day to open it like view 1.
