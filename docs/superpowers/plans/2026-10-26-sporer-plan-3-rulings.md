# Sporer Plan 3 — rulings, parked findings and checks before release

These are the decisions made on the owner's behalf while building Plan 3: home and trips, the trips list, the trip view and the house icon. The plan was built task by task, with a separate review of each task, then a whole-branch review and one fix wave. Each decision states what it costs if it turns out wrong. Generated from the build ledger on 2026-10-27.

## Check with real data before the first `v*` tag

The owner's checklist in the README ("Trips and home") is the acceptance test. In short:

- **Home periods.** They look right, including any move. Nearby places that were 03:00 locations count as one home (`Detection__HomeRadiusMeters`, 250 m). A move under about 500 m may not show.
- **Trips.** Every trip you remember is listed, with sensible dates, name and nights. A long stay is not mistaken for a move.
- **House icon.** Home stops show a house and nothing else does: a café next door keeps its number.
- **Trip view.** The day lines read well. The trip view and the trips list are quick on the phone.

These cannot be verified in the build container (no WebKit and no real data):

- The house icon is a signed-distance-field image drawn by MapLibre. Check that it renders crisply on iOS Safari.
- City labels on the trips and trip maps face inward and are not cut off at the screen edge on the phone.
- The shell's `overflow: clip`: a focused or tapped card in a moving sheet never scrolls the whole layout.
- Performance on real data. The targets were measured only on synthetic data with software WebGL (see `2026-10-26-sporer-plan-3-perf.md`).
- Upgrading from the current release rebuilds the whole timeline once on first start, because the algorithm version and options changed.

## Residual findings after the final fix wave (parked)

- A trip that ends at a new home still draws its thumbnail back to the old home (P3-R34).
- `sheetAtRest` in the e2e helpers can return before a transition starts after `goBack()` (P3-R34).
